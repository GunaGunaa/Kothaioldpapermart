## 🚀 Key Features

1. **Modern Layout Architecture:**
    - **Top Floating Action Bar:** Pinned and sticky at the very top even when scrolling, holding instant access to `WhatsApp Quote` and `Book Pickup`.
    - **Dedicated Brand Title & Logo Showcase:** Grand standalone presentation with a prominent logo emblem and extra-big font title (`KOTHAI OLD PAPER MART`) in a single horizontal line.
    - **Dedicated Category Navigation Section:** Clean horizontal pill bar for `Home`, `Scrap Rates`, `Price Calculator`, `What We Buy`, `How It Works`, `Commercial`, `Contact`.
    - 100% mobile-first and responsive across all devices (Smartphones, Tablets, Laptops, Desktops).

2. **Interactive Live Scrap Value Calculator:**
    - Real-time calculator where customers can pick a scrap category (Newspaper, Cardboard, Copper, Brass, Aluminium, Iron, Batteries, ACs, Refrigerators, E-Waste) and adjust weight/quantity via a slider to get instant estimated payouts in INR (₹).
    - Direct 1-click booking with pre-filled estimate.

3. **Filterable & Searchable Live Scrap Rate Card:**
    - Category filters (Paper & Cardboard, Metals, Appliances & E-Waste, Batteries & Plastics).
    - Real-time search bar to look up specific items (e.g., "copper", "newspaper", "battery").
    - "Sell This" direct trigger on every card.

4. **Doorstep Pickup Booking System:**
    - **Option A:** Instant WhatsApp 1-Click Booking (formats item list, address, date slot, and launches WhatsApp chat).
    - **Option B:** Online Booking Form with instant confirmation modal and slot scheduler.

5. **Local Business SEO & Rich Structured Data:**
    - Schema.org `RecyclingCenter` / `LocalBusiness` JSON-LD structured data with geo-coordinates, updated address in Yarandahalli, hours, phone, and accepted payment modes.
    - OpenGraph and Twitter cards for social sharing.

6. **Customer Conversion Drivers:**
    - Sticky mobile bottom bar (Call Now, WhatsApp, Book Pickup, Rate Card).
    - Trust highlights (Certified digital scale, Instant UPI/Cash payment, Free doorstep pickup, Zero-landfill recycling).
    - Traditional scrap dealer vs. Kothai Old Paper Mart comparison table.
    - Commercial & Industrial bulk scrap solutions for IT parks in Electronic City, Bommasandra, and Jigani.
    - Customer testimonials and Interactive FAQ accordion.
    - Interactive Google Maps embed with direct navigation directions.
